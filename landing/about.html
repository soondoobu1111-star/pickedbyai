About pickedby.ai

The AI Visibility Score. Measure. Diagnose. Improve.

WHAT WE DO

pickedby.ai checks whether the five major AI engines — GPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, and Perplexity — actually recommend your product when someone asks. You get a 0–100 score across four dimensions and a prescription engine that returns specific, rule-based actions to improve.

It's not a scale. It's a personal trainer for AI visibility.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Direct Recognition (35 pts) — does Gemini and Perplexity recognize your product by name
  • Category Ranking (35 pts) — does your product appear in "best {category}" AI top-10
  • Co-Recommendation (20 pts) — how often AI mentions you alongside category leaders
  • Web Authority (10 pts) — coverage on Tier-1 and Tier-2 sources AI crawls

Sum equals final. No black-box weighting. Methodology details in the scoring writeup.
