What your score does tell you is how visible your product is to AI right now — and whether you have a realistic chance of showing up when customers ask AI for recommendations.

Think of it like SEO: good optimization improves your chances, but doesn't guarantee page 1. Same idea here.

AI learns from what's publicly available on the internet. If your product has a low score, it usually means one of these:

  • Not enough public content — reviews, blog posts, forum mentions about your product are sparse
  • Vague product description — AI doesn't have a clear category or use-case to attach to your product
  • New product — AI training data hasn't caught up yet
  • No llms.txt or schema markup — AI crawlers can't easily parse what your product does

After your scan, we show you specific suggestions tailored to your score. These are your starting points.

After your scan you'll see personalized tips. Generally the most effective moves are:

  • Write clear product copy — state exactly what your product does, who it's for, and when to use it
  • Get mentioned in context — Reddit threads, YouTube reviews, blog posts, comparison articles all feed AI training data
  • Add an llms.txt file to your site — a simple text file that AI crawlers can read directly (auto-generation coming soon in the Dashboard)
  • Get on curated lists — "Best Notion templates for X" type roundups are gold for AI visibility
  • Consistent naming — use the same product name everywhere, so AI can connect the dots
